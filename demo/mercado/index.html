How it works

From a stuck negotiation to a shared opportunity.

01 / DETECT

Your agent finds a lead

It reads your accessible negotiations and public markets, focusing on failed or stalled deals. You authorize the selected information it shares.

02 / CONNECT

Mercado finds a route

It connects compatible intentions from at least three teams, preferring fewer participants. You see your own part, not everyone’s private proposal.

03 / APPROVE & TRADE

Everyone buys in

You approve, or authorize your agent within explicit limits. Then local agents create and accept the offers in Mercado, with Bazaar moving cards and cash.

One important limit: the route consists of separate trades, so it can complete partially. Each trade must be safe on its own. Unknown values or conflicting evidence stop execution for human review.
